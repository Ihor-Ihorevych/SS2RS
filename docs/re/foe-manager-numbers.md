## Summary

The foe manager runs **two self-scheduled loops**, one per state. This page gives the numbers and the exact order of both, and the rules that the earlier pages left open. What an implementer needs first:

1. **Both loops sleep for `CalculateSleepTime`** (section 2): the 3-D distance `d` from the brain's puppet to the **nearest puppet that may be its foe** (any puppet that passes the alignment test, not only players); no such puppet: **1.0 s**; `d` above the `.cb`'s **active range**: **5.0 s**; `d` below the **threat range**: **0.1 s**; between the two: **0.1 s + 0.4 s x clamp((d - threat) / (active - threat), 0, 1)**. The looking loop uses **1e-5 s** instead while a delayed assignment is pending (section 3). The first run of either loop comes 1e-5 s after the state is entered.
2. **A foe seen, sensed or heard (hostility 0 to 2) is assigned only after a reaction delay** when the brain has no foe yet (section 3): `(cb_fInitialReactionPeriod, or 0.5 s when it is negative) x the difficulty's dif_fInitialDelayMultiplier` (Tourist 1.5 ... Serious 0.25). All 82 `.cb` files use the global 0.5 s, so the delay is **0.75 / 0.625 / 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.125 s** by difficulty. Higher hostilities are assigned at once.
3. **`en_lafLastAssignedFoe`** is `LAF_NONE` 0, `LAF_NON_PLAYER` 1, `LAF_PLAYER` 2: whether the **last assigned foe was a player brain**. It drives the evil puppets' preference for players (sections 2.1 and 2.3) and a refusal rule of damage assignment (section 3).
4. **`FH_NEAR`** (5) can replace a `FH_NEAR` foe only after **2.0 s** (section 3); the near-foe switch of the tracking loop fires when the nearest possible foe is **closer than the threat range**.
5. **Candidate sort key** (section 4): 3-D distance, ascending, **compared as the integer part of the difference**, so candidates less than 1 unit apart tie and the order inside a tie is the C library sort's (not stable).
6. **"May be foes"** (section 5): the alignment table, plus the player-invisible cheat, plus (**only** for the damage route `OnBrainDamaged`) a hostility branch with a per-puppet foe table that is **empty in all 124 puppet files**.
7. **Corrections** of earlier pages are collected in section 8; the main ones: the periods (the earlier text said 1e-5 s on the common path), the scream (it names the screamer's **foe**), the threat-sound origin (the **eye point**, not the placement) and the radius member of the scream.

## Layout / rules

### 1. Time and scheduling (**medium**)

- **Time values** are 64-bit fixed-point numbers with **2^-32 s** resolution; every elapsed time below is `now - stored time` converted to a float number of seconds (the conversion divides by 4,294,967,296). Comparisons "has passed" are **strict**: `elapsed > limit`.
- **Scheduled calls.** The foe manager does not run in the entity step. It asks the entity base for "call my method `name` after `t` seconds" (a replaceable call: a new request replaces the pending one, `SetState` first cancels it). The **minimum period of the engine, 1e-5 s**, is what every "next instant" request passes.
- The two scheduled methods are `LookForFoe` (state `FMS_LOOKING_FOR_FOE`) and `TrackCurrentFoe` (state `FMS_TRACKING_CURRENT_FOE`). **Entering a state schedules its method 1e-5 s later** (`SetState`: the looking state through `BeginLookingForFoe`, which also tells the world info entity that the brain needs a foe; the tracking state directly). `FoeLost` clears `en_henFoe` and `en_henToBeFoe`, tells the brain `OnFoeLost` and enters the looking state again, so a lost foe restarts the looking loop after 1e-5 s. A loop that changes the state returns **without** rescheduling itself.

### 2. The loops and their periods

#### 2.1 `LookForFoe` (state looking; **medium**; the order of `brain-senses.md` 4 with these corrections)

1. **Player priority** (unchanged): the manager is not auto-changing (`en_bAutoChangesFoes` false), the puppet's alignment is evil (1) and `en_lafLastAssignedFoe` is `LAF_NON_PLAYER` (1): `SwitchToClosestPlayer` (section 4); when it assigns a player the manager is put into the tracking state and the call ends. So **an evil puppet whose last foe was not a player always prefers the nearest player on its next search**.
2. Candidate (`GetPossibleFoe`, section 4), `en_bHasFoeCandidates`, sensitivity below 3 and `en_henLastTestedFoe`: as in `brain-senses.md` 4 steps 2 and 3, with this precision: **`en_henLastTestedFoe` is written only when a candidate exists and the threat sensitivity is 3 or more** (a sensitivity-1 or -2 puppet leaves it unchanged; `en_bHasFoeCandidates` is written either way).
3. Ranges, field of view, see distance and the line-of-sight request: as in `brain-senses.md` 4 steps 4 to 7 (the request is the engine's line-of-sight service, specified with T01-244: a ray from this puppet to the candidate's puppet with the collision category `character_sees_character`; "seen" means the ray reaches the target point or its first hit is the target itself; **its answer is delivered to `OnSeeTarget` / `OnNotSeeTarget` inside the same call**, so the sequence "request, then `OnSeeTarget` assigns the foe" happens before the next step; if the answer changed the manager's state the call ends).
4. **Rescheduling** (corrects `brain-senses.md` 4 step 8 and `behavior-entity-classes.md` 7.3). At the end of every call that did not end early:
   - `en_henToBeFoe` is **empty**: schedule the next call after **`CalculateSleepTime`** seconds;
   - `en_henToBeFoe` is set and the clock is **past `en_tmAutoAssignFoe`**: `AssignFoeNow(en_henToBeFoe)`, clear `en_henToBeFoe`, and end (the assignment enters the tracking state);
   - `en_henToBeFoe` is set and the time has not come: schedule after **1e-5 s** (the manager polls at the engine's fastest rate until the reaction delay is over, section 3).

#### 2.2 `CalculateSleepTime` (**medium**; constants read from the data section, branch directions checked in the listing)

Inputs: the **nearest foe puppet** `N` of the brain's puppet (`CWorldInfoEntity::GetClosestFoePuppet`: scan **all** puppets of the world info list, keep those for which `CanBecomeMyEnemy(my puppet, it, false)` is true, take the smallest 3-D distance; none: no result) and the puppet's `.cb` with `cb_fActiveRange` `A` and `cb_fThreatRange` `T` (the sense range is not used). `d` = the 3-D distance between the two **placements** (positions, not eye points).

| Case (tested in this order) | Result (seconds) |
|---|---|
| no `N` | **1.0** |
| `d > A` | **5.0** |
| `d < T` | **0.1** |
| otherwise | `0.1 + 0.4 * clamp((d - T) / (A - T), 0, 1)` |

- A puppet with **no `.cb` object** gets `A = T = 0`: result 5.0 unless `d` is exactly 0 (then 0.1).
- When `A == T` and `d == A` the quotient is 0/0 (not a number) and the code returns a not-a-number period: **no data file has A equal to T** (script section 1), so the case is unobserved. When `T > A` (two `.cb` files: A 2,500, T 5,000) the middle branch is unreachable: the result is **0.1 up to A and 5.0 beyond**.
- The value is the same function for both loops. In the data (script section 1) the (A, T) pairs are (250, 10) 59 files, (2,500, 50) 8, (2,500, 10) 3, (250, 25) 3, (250, 0) 3, (2,500, 5,000) 2, (250, 20) 2, (25,000, 10) 1, (400, 10) 1: a puppet with the common pair sleeps **0.1 s** within 10 m of the nearest foe puppet, 0.1 to 0.5 s from 10 m to 250 m, and **5 s** beyond 250 m.
- **Which `.cb` member is which range** is now settled (T01-244's question): the three ranges sit in the order active, sense, threat in the object, and the three code paths that read them use the members whose names are `cb_fActiveRange` (the 3-D "ignore this candidate" limit of the search, and the far end of the sleep slope), `cb_fSenseRange` (`FH_SENSED`) and `cb_fThreatRange` (`FH_NEAR` and the near end of the slope). Basis: the member table gives the offsets of the three members and of `cb_fInitialReactionPeriod`, and the offsets used by the code match the table's **by offset**, not by order (**medium**: one table, one reading per use).

#### 2.3 `TrackCurrentFoe` (state tracking; **medium**)

Each call, in order:

1. **Auto-lose.** When `en_fAutoLoseFoePeriod` is not -1: if `now - en_tmFoeAssigned > en_fAutoLoseFoePeriod` the foe is lost (`FoeLost`); when the foe is then really gone the period member is reset to -1, and **the call ends without rescheduling** either way. (Every data route passes -1: `OnBrainDamaged`, `HuntFoe`, `OnTouchedByBrain`, `OnSeeTarget`, `SwitchToClosestPlayer`, the near switch; only `ForceFoe` and the script action can pass another value.)
2. **20-second player switch.** When the puppet's alignment is evil, **the world info entity's registered boss puppet is not this puppet** (the boss registered with `RegisterBoss`, `bosses.md`), `en_bAutoChangesFoes` is false, `en_lafLastAssignedFoe` is `LAF_NON_PLAYER` and `now - en_tmFoeAssigned > 20.0 s`: `SwitchToClosestPlayer` (the result is ignored; if it assigned a player, `AssignFoeNow` has just re-entered the tracking state and **the call goes on with the new foe**; its own reschedule at the end replaces the one that `SetState` made). So **a non-boss evil puppet that has fought a non-player for more than 20 s changes to the nearest player**.
3. **Foe check.** The foe must still exist and its puppet must answer "valid" to the puppet's own validity question (the foe's puppet exists and is alive; **medium-low**, the callee is a small virtual question). When it does **not**: the brain is told `CelebrateFoeDied` when the world info entity says this brain still has a possible foe (`DoIHavePossibleFoe`), else `CelebrateAllFoesDied`, then `FoeLost` (and the call ends). (These two are AI-moment raisers of the brain, `brain-classes.md` 3.2; T01-126 owns their moments.)
4. **Sight of the foe.** Threat sensitivity **8** (see through walls): no request; `en_tmFoeLastSeenTime` := now, `en_vFoeLastSeenPos` := the foe puppet's position, `en_bFoeSeenOnLastTest` := true. Otherwise one **line-of-sight request** from this puppet to the foe's puppet (`brain-line-of-sight.md`), answered inside the call by `OnSeeTarget` (records time, position, flag) or `OnNotSeeTarget` (clears the flag; the 60 s rule below); **if that changed the state** (the 60 s rule lost the foe) the call ends.
5. **Near switch.** `N` := `GetClosestFoePuppet` of this puppet (section 2.2); when `N` exists, its brain is **not** the current foe and `3-D distance < cb_fThreatRange`: `AssignFoe(N's brain, FH_NEAR, -1)` (accepted by the rank and the 2.0 s rule of section 3).
6. **Scream** (once per foe): when `en_bScreamed` is false and `now - en_tmFoeAssigned > 1.0 + en_fRnd` (strictly; `en_fRnd` of this puppet, section 6): `en_bScreamed` := true and **the puppet emanates a threat sound**: every brain in the world info's "needs a foe" list whose distance to this puppet's placement is **strictly less than `ggp_fPuppetThreatDistance`** (100 in the data) runs `ThreatSoundHeard` with the **current foe's brain** as the emitter brain and **this puppet** as the emitter entity (see section 8: the hearers learn about the screamer's foe and walk towards the screamer).
7. **Reschedule** after **`CalculateSleepTime`**.

**`OnNotSeeTarget` 60-second rule** (unchanged, checked): in the tracking state, when `now - en_tmFoeLastSeenTime > 60.0 s` (the constant is a literal) and the foe's hostility is not `FH_HUNTING` (8), the foe is lost. `en_tmFoeLastSeenTime` starts at the **assignment time** (`AssignFoe` stores the time, the foe's position and a seen flag), so the rule counts from the assignment when the foe is never seen. The rule is evaluated only when an answer "not seen" arrives, i.e. once per tracking call, so its precision is the sleep time (up to 5 s).

### 3. `AssignFoe(candidate, hostility, autoLosePeriod)` in full (**medium**; supersedes `behavior-entity-classes.md` 7.3 where they differ)

In order:

1. Return when the candidate handle is empty or the manager is `FMS_DISABLED`.
2. **Same foe:** when the candidate is the current foe: raise the stored hostility if the new one is higher, store `autoLosePeriod`, return.
3. **Refusals.** The candidate is a **player brain** and `en_bCantAssignPlayerAsFoe` is set: return; the candidate's puppet is a **player puppet with the invisible cheat on** (a bit of the player's cheat-flag word that the invisible cheat toggles, section 5): return; `en_bCantAssignNonPlayerAsFoe` is set and the candidate is **not** a player brain: return.
4. **Rank rule.** `replace` is true when: there is no current foe, or the new hostility is 9, or both are `FH_NEAR` (5), or the new hostility is **higher** than the stored one. The **damage exception**: when this puppet's alignment is evil, `en_lafLastAssignedFoe` is `LAF_NON_PLAYER`, the new hostility is `FH_DAMAGED` (4) and the stored hostility is not 6, 8 or 9, the assignment is accepted **whatever `replace` says** (an evil puppet fighting a non-player always switches to whoever damaged it, unless it is forced, hunting or auto-changed). In every other case a false `replace` returns.
5. **The `FH_NEAR` interval.** A `FH_NEAR` request against a current `FH_NEAR` foe is refused when `now - en_tmNearFoeAssigned < 2.0 s`; every accepted `FH_NEAR` request stores `en_tmNearFoeAssigned` := now.
6. **Record** (these stores happen for every accepted request, also for a delayed one): `en_fhFoeHostility` := hostility; `en_tmFoeLastSeenTime` := now; `en_vFoeLastSeenPos` := the candidate's position; `en_bFoeSeenOnLastTest` := true; `en_fAutoLoseFoePeriod` := `autoLosePeriod`; `en_bHasHadFoe` := true.
7. **Delayed or immediate.** When there is **no current foe and the hostility is below 3** (`FH_LIGHT`, `FH_HEARD`, `FH_SEEN`): if `en_henToBeFoe` is empty, set it to the candidate and set `en_tmAutoAssignFoe` := now + **delay**, where delay = (`cb_fInitialReactionPeriod` when the `.cb` exists and it is 0 or more, else `ggp_fDefaultInitialDelay` = 0.5 s) x the current difficulty's `dif_fInitialDelayMultiplier` (Tourist 1.5, Easy 1.25, Normal 1, Hard 0.5, Serious 0.25: `player-rules.md`); the looking loop makes the assignment when the time comes (section 2.1). If `en_henToBeFoe` is already set the request is **dropped** (the first candidate keeps the slot). In every other case `AssignFoeNow(candidate)` runs.
8. **`AssignFoeNow`**: stores the foe, tells the brain `OnFoeAssigned` and `AdjustClientOrientation`, sets `en_lafLastAssignedFoe` := `LAF_PLAYER` when the foe's brain is a player brain else `LAF_NON_PLAYER`, tells the world info entity the brain has a foe, `en_tmFoeAssigned` := now, `en_bScreamed` := false and enters the tracking state. (`CBrainEntity::OnFoeAssigned` of the brain: sets `en_bFoeAssignedBefore` and `en_bImmediateThinkPoint` on the **first** foe of the brain's life, section 6.)

**Who calls it with which rank** (all with period -1 unless stated): `OnSeeTarget` in the looking state `FH_SEEN`; the sound code `FH_HEARD` or `FH_SEEN`; `OnBrainDamaged` `FH_DAMAGED`; `OnTouchedByBrain` `FH_TOUCHED` (7); `HuntFoe` `FH_HUNTING` (8); `ForceFoe` `FH_FORCED` (6) with a period; `SwitchToClosestPlayer` `FH_AUTO_CHANGED_FOE` (9); the near switch `FH_NEAR`; `LookForFoe` `FH_SENSED` and sensitivity-8 `FH_SEEN`; `TrySwitchingToAnotherFoe`: the rank it passes was not resolved (open question 1). `OnBrainDamaged` requires the foe manager not disabled, a valid damager and the puppet's threat sensitivity **above 1** (`TS_DEADHORSE` ignores damage); `HuntFoe` and `OnTouchedByBrain` require only the alignment rule.

### 4. Candidate lists and the sort (**medium**)

- **`GetFoeCandidates(my puppet, except, playersOnly, out)`**: every puppet of the world info list except `except`, kept when (not `playersOnly` or it is a player puppet) and `CanBecomeMyEnemy(my puppet, it, false)`.
- **`GetSortedFoeCandidates(list, maxDistance, playersOnly, excludeCurrentFoe)`**: the candidates above (`except` = the current foe's puppet when `excludeCurrentFoe` and a foe exists), kept when the 3-D distance to this puppet is **at most** `maxDistance`, then **sorted ascending by distance** with the C library's `qsort` over (puppet handle, distance) pairs. The comparator returns the **integer truncation of `distance(a) - distance(b)`** (toward zero): two candidates whose distances differ by **less than 1.0** are "equal" and their order is whatever the sort leaves (not stable). Implementers who need the exact order inside such ties must use the same sort algorithm; for gameplay it is "nearest first within 1 unit".
- **`SwitchToClosestPlayer`**: does nothing (returns false) when `en_bCantAssignPlayerAsFoe`; else `GetSortedFoeCandidates(list, 3.0e38, playersOnly = true, excludeCurrentFoe = false)`; when the list is not empty it calls `AssignFoe(first entry's brain, FH_AUTO_CHANGED_FOE, -1)` and returns true. (So the first entry is the nearest player puppet that passes `CanBecomeMyEnemy`; if the player is already the foe the call is the "same foe" case of section 3 and returns true all the same.)
- **`TrySwitchingToAnotherFoe`** (after an attack behaviour, `behavior-generic-puppet.md` 7): `maxDistance` = **1.5 x the 3-D distance to the current foe** (the function is called with a foe; without one the code leaves its initial large constant, which is not specified here); the candidates exclude the current foe; for each in order a line-of-sight request is made (**synchronous result, no receiver**); the first that is visible is assigned with `AssignFoe` and the function returns true; none: false. **The multiplier is 1.5** (read as a float constant).
- **`GetPossibleFoe`** (section 4 of `brain-senses.md`): the scan is **cyclic over the list**: start index = (position of `en_henLastTestedFoe`'s puppet in the list) + 1 when it is in the list, else `floor(u * count)` with `u` a fresh random float in [0, 1); then `count` steps with the index taken **modulo `count`**; the first puppet that is a puppet, whose brain is not the excluded one (`LookForFoe` passes none) and that passes `CanBecomeMyEnemy(my puppet, it, false)` is returned; none: an empty handle.

### 5. `CanBecomeMyEnemy(a, b, hostilityFlag)` (**medium**; fills `brain-senses.md` 6)

False when: either puppet is missing; `a` is `b`; **both have the same non-empty `en_idPuppet`** (the character id); `b` is a **player puppet whose invisible-cheat bit is set**. Then:

- **`hostilityFlag` false** (every caller except one): the result is `CanBeEnemyToMyAlignment(a, b)` (the 4 x 4 table of `brain-senses.md` 7).
- **`hostilityFlag` true**: **only `OnBrainDamaged` passes true** (the other seven call sites that were read pass false: the three candidate scans of the world info entity (`GetPossibleFoe`, `GetFoeCandidates`, `DoIHavePossibleFoe`), `GetClosestFoePuppet`, `HuntFoe`, `OnTouchedByBrain` and `ThreatSoundHeard`). The hostility branch is tried first: when the global `gpp_bEnemiesCanFightEachother` (1 in the data) is set, **both puppets have a parameter file** and **at least one has `en_bCanAttackOwnAligment`**: the answer is **yes** when `gpp_bAllEnemiesShouldFightEachother` (0 in the data) is set, or when **either** puppet's foe table accepts the other's `en_idPuppet`; if none of that holds, the alignment rule decides. A foe table (`pp_faaFoeAssigning` of the puppet's parameter file) has a mode and a list of character ids: **an id that is in the list gives `FTI_INCLUDE` (1) true and `FTI_EXCLUDE` (0) false; an id that is not in the list gives `FTI_EXCLUDE` true and `FTI_INCLUDE` false.** **All 124 puppet files have mode `FTI_EXCLUDE` and an empty list** (script section 2), so in the data the table **accepts everybody**: with the global switch on (it is), a damaged puppet may turn on a damager of the **same alignment** exactly when one of the two has `en_bCanAttackOwnAligment` (script section 3 counts the placed puppets that have it).
- The invisible cheat: the player puppet's cheat-flag word has a bit (value 8) that the "Cheat - Invisible" command toggles; `CanBecomeMyEnemy` and `AssignFoe` refuse such a player (**medium**: the bit was identified by the toggle function).

### 6. The think period and `en_fRnd` (**medium**; completes `behavior-action-choice.md` 7)

- **The formula of the oscillator is confirmed unchanged** (re-read, every constant checked): no foe or no band: `x = 1 + u`; with a foe and a band: `x` := the band graph at the area ratio (when the graph exists), times `1 + 2 (en_fRnd - 0.5) * cba_fActionPeriodRandomizeMultiplier`, divided by the current difficulty's `dif_fThinkFrequencyMultiplier`; the delay is `max(1e-5, last action time + x - now)`. The difficulty record is read from the game info entity (think multiplier and initial-delay multiplier are two members of the same record).
- **`en_fRnd`** is drawn **once, in the puppet's constructor**, from the engine's global random generator: a 32-bit unsigned random number times 2^-32 as a float, so **uniform in [0, 1]** (the exact value 1.0 occurs only through float rounding of the largest 128 integers: probability 2^-25). It is a **replicated** member, never redrawn, **not saved**, and the **player puppet sets it to 0** in `OnCreate`, so a player's own `en_fRnd` is 0 and the factor above is `1 - cba_fActionPeriodRandomizeMultiplier`.
- **The immediate think point.** The brain's `en_bFoeAssignedBefore` and `en_bImmediateThinkPoint` are cleared when the brain boots and set together by `OnFoeAssigned` on the **first foe assignment of the brain's life**; the oscillator's next `ExecuteBehavior` then **clears the flag and schedules the next think after 1e-5 s** instead of computing the period (so the first foe triggers one immediate think, in addition to the oscillator's own `OnFoeAssigned` reschedule). `OnFoeAssigned` also reschedules an existing oscillator immediately and raises an AI moment (`brain-classes.md` 3.2).
- `cb_pgrThinkPeriodOveride` is **not** read by the oscillator (confirmed): `behavior-action-choice.md` 7.

### 7. Data (script `tools/re/foe_manager_verify.py`; **high**)

- **Auto-change chance** (`fad_fAutoChangeFoeChance`): 0 in 58 files, 0.25 in 2, 0.5 in 1, 1 in 1, 1.5 in 1, 2 in 11, 3 in 7, 10 in 1: **`en_bAutoChangesFoes` is on for 24 behaviours and off for 58**; the 58 are the puppets for which the player preference of sections 2.1 and 2.3 applies (when they are evil).

- **Own-alignment attacks:** of 2,328 placed puppets **1** has `en_bCanAttackOwnAligment` set (an evil puppet of sensitivity 8, `TS_SEETHROUGHWALLS`). The member can also be changed at run time (a spawner tweak or a script macro; not counted here), so the hostility branch is rare in the data.

## Invariants

1. The sleep time is always one of: 1.0 (no foe puppet), 5.0, 0.1, or a value in [0.1, 0.5] (script section 4 vectors); it is never below 0.1 s, never 1e-5 s, except in the looking loop with a pending delayed assignment.

3. An evil, non-auto-changing puppet never keeps a non-player foe in the tracking state for longer than 20 s plus one sleep period, unless it is the registered boss or no player passes the alignment test (sections 2.1 and 2.3).
4. In the data no puppet file has a non-empty foe table, so the hostility branch of `CanBecomeMyEnemy` never says "no" because of a table (124 of 124 files).
