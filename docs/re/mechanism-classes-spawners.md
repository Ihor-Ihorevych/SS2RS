## Summary

A **spawner** is the entity that **creates a character, item, prop or multi-spawner** in the level: it owns a **template copy of the spawnee's property record**, delays, counts, a spawn placement, optionally a **formation**, a **launcher** and a **spawn effect**, and reports progress to the level script through ten events. **3,783 spawners in 46 worlds** (`SSpawnerEntity`, 74 named methods, 59 members) make most of the level's enemies and a third of its items; **60 multi-spawners** (`CMultiSpawnerEntity`) hold a list of alternative templates; `CSpawnDelayerEntity` (a run-time helper that delays a spawn effect) and the **177 spawn markers** (`CSpawnMarkerEntity`) complete the family.

What a reader needs to know first:

1. **Structure in the data** (`wld-entities.md`, checked here again): a spawner has no serial and no world record; `.120` (`en_henOwner`) is the spawnee entity of the level, `.21` (`en_hepSpawnProperties`) a **copy of the spawnee's record** that is used to make the copies, and the spawnee's own record points at an **owned `SSpawnerProperties`** record with the spawner's design numbers. 3,783 of 3,783 spawners follow it. The spawnee is a character (2,195), an item (1,492), a multi-spawner (60) or a prop (36).
2. **Counts and groups.** Design numbers: `ep_ctTotal` (how many to make in all), `ep_ctGroup` (the group size), delays (`ep_fDelayInitial`, `ep_fDelaySingle`, `ep_fDelayGroup`, `ep_fSpawneeDeathDelay`). The **events** use the counts: `OneSpawned` every *multiplier* spawnees, `GroupSpawned` every group, `AllSpawned` at the total, the same for `...Killed` and `...Picked` (section 4).
3. **The spawner boots with a "multiplier"** that scales the number of enemies by the session's enemy multiplier (co-op) with per-class and per-level rules, and applies the **tweak tables** (`mechanism-classes-spawner-tweaks.md`). The scaled counts replace the design numbers: the entity stores `en_ctInitialTotal`, `en_ctInitialGroup` and `en_iMultiplier` (the multiplier, 1 in all stored entities).
4. **Spawning is limited by the world info entity**: three **spawn slot** containers (evil, good, misc) with a limit per chapter; a spawner waits for a free slot (`mechanism-classes-world-info.md`).
5. **Host only.** The spawner has no net behaviour function; it exists and runs on the host (`NEB_NONE`, `replication.md`); clients see only the spawned entities (each carries `en_henSpawnerWhoSpawnedMe`).

### 3. Spawnee count limit type

`en_scltSpawneeCountLimitType` (`ESpawneeCountLimitType`) says **which spawn slot container** the spawnee counts against (`CWorldInfoEntity::GetWaitArray` and `IsSpawningAllowed`, read; the waiting containers are declared in the order evil, good, misc and the chapter's three limits in the order good, evil, misc): **2 = "good"** (limit `ep_ctMaxGoodSpawneesAtOnce` of the chapter), **3 = "evil"** (`ep_ctMaxEvilSpawneesAtOnce`), **4 = "misc"** (`ep_ctMaxMiscSpawneesAtOnce`); the value 1 means "not limited" (the check returns at once; no spawner has it). In the data (script, cross-tabulation, all 3,783 spawners): **3 (evil): 1,927 characters and 58 multi-spawners; 4 (misc): all 1,492 items, 36 props, 225 characters and 2 multi-spawners; 2 (good): 43 characters**. The evil limit is 10,000 (effectively none) in 301 of 307 chapters and 10 to 30 in the other 6 (`mechanism-classes-world-info.md`). Which characters are "misc" or "good" (neutral and friendly puppets by their alignment: a reading of the multiplier code) is not established (low).

### 4. Counters and the ten events (read; medium to high: three functions with the same formula)

Let *m* = `en_iMultiplier`, *group* = `en_ctInitialGroup` and *total* = `en_ctTotal`, which `CommonEntryPoint` sets to `en_ctInitialTotal * m` when the procedure starts (read).
- **Spawn side** (`CountOneSpawned`, called after each spawnee exists): increment `en_ctSpawned` by 1; **`OneSpawned`** if `spawned mod m = 0`; **`GroupSpawned`** if `(spawned mod group) * m = 0`; **`AllSpawned`** if `spawned = total`. If the spawner counts against the evil container (type 3) and is not a death spawner, it is **unregistered from the spawn slots** when all are spawned (read).
- **Kill side** (`OnSpawneeKilled(spawnee)`, called when a spawnee dies): increment the killed counter by 1 and raise **`OneKilled`** (`killed mod m = 0`), **`GroupKilled`** (`(killed mod group) * m = 0`) and **`AllKilled`** (`killed = total`); then the spawner's own virtual `OnSpawneeKilledInternalNotification` runs (not read).
- **Pick side** (`OnSpawneePicked(item)`, for an item spawnee picked up): the function releases the spawner's spawn slot, keeps the group counter at least 1 and counts the pick like a kill, with the `Picked` events (**`OnePicked`, `GroupPicked`, `AllPicked`**; read in part, the counting formula was not read to the end); `SendAmmoItemEvents` raises the same three events for **ammunition items** (profiled). `ShouldntSpawnAmmoItems` (profiled) returns true for a spawner whose template is a serious bomb item class when the session setting that disables serious bombs is on (low).
- **Spawn source dead** (`OnSpawnSourceDied`): when a spawner's source (a dropship, a prop) is destroyed, **every spawnee not yet made counts as killed** at once: the kill events are raised for each missing one, the counters are set to the total and the spawner ends (state 2).
- **`SpawneeAvailable`** is raised on the spawner by `SpawnOneSpawnee` after the spawnee has been made (profiled: the string is in that function); the script then calls `GetLastSpawned` (`level-logic.md`: 512 handlers in 41 worlds).
The compare "`mod m`" is why the **events are thinned** when the spawner is multiplied: with *m* = 4 a handler that waits for `OneKilled` is called every fourth death, so a script designed for one enemy sees the same number of events with four times the enemies.

### 5. Spawning procedures, states and members of the spawn

- **Entry points.** The spawner has three **procedures** (macro state functions): `SimpleSpawnerEntryPoint` (make the spawnees one by one with the single delay), `MaintainGroupSpawnerEntryPoint` (keep a group alive: after a spawnee dies wait `ep_fSpawneeDeathDelay` and make the next) and `RespawnByGroupSpawnerEntryPoint` (make a whole group, wait for all to die, make the next group): `en_bGroupSpawner` and `en_bChangeToSimple` choose (low). Common code: `CommonEntryPoint` (profiled and partly read) checks that the group count is positive ("Group count peroperty is invalid (<=0). Can't spawn."), that spawning is not paused ("Spawning paused.") and that spawn properties exist ("No spawn properties. Can't spawn."), computes `total = ctInitialTotal * multiplier`, registers with the world info, and then drives `SpawnEntityProcedure`, `SpawnSourceAndSpawnEffect`, `SpawnOneSpawnee`, `WaitFreeSpawnSlot`, `WaitSpawneeDeathDelay`, `WaitAllSpawneesToDie`, `WaitThenSendAmmoItemEvents`, `SkipSpawning`, `DelaySingleAndSpawn` and `SpawnReady` as **latent state functions** (the eleven names in `SetState`).
- **`SpawnEntityProcedure`** (read): if the spawner is not in the "limit" state it enters it; then, unless `en_bIgnoreMax` is set (the "ignore max" tweak), it asks the world info `IsSpawningAllowed(spawner)`; when **not allowed** the spawner enters the **wait state (15)** and is woken by `OnSpawnSlotAvailable` when a slot frees.
- **States.** `SpawnerState` is 0 at the start in all 3,783; the code uses **1 to 15** (`SetState` jumps into a state function for 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14 and treats 15 as the wait state; 2 is "finished"); the eleven state function names are listed above; **the numbers are established in [`spawner-state-machine.md`](spawner-state-machine.md) section 2** (high).
- **The spawn itself** (`SpawnOneSpawnee`, profiled, 1.9 kB): copies the template record with its links (`epCopyEntityPropertiesWithLinks`), creates the entity (`SpawnEntity`), sets its placement from the spawn placement `en_qvSpawnOrigin` (or the formation's marker, or the launcher's start), gives it the velocity from `GetRandomSpawnVelocity` or the launcher (`LaunchEntity`), counts it (`CountOneSpawned`), applies the spawner's health (`en_iHealth` on a character) and flags, plays the spawn effect (`DoSpawnEffect`: builds an effect object and, for an effect with a delay, a **`CSpawnDelayerProperties`/entity** that makes the spawnee after the effect's `sfx_fSpawnDelay`) and raises `SpawneeAvailable`. A spawnee that cannot be made logs "Spawner::SpawnOneSpawnee(): Entity can't be spawned even though spawn properties are valid".
- **Launchers** (`SRelativeRandomConeLauncher` 524: a cone relative to the spawner, five numbers; `SMarkersLauncher` 166: fly towards a marker with a pitch-over-distance graph) and **formations** (`CMarkersFormation` 879: places at marker entities; `CLineFormation` 288; `CRectFormation` 87; `CVFormation` 26; `CPieSliceFormation` 15) are data classes of `classes-catalog.md`; `AdjustForLauncherAndFormationParams` (1.5 kB, profiled), `SetupLauncherPreview` and `SetupSpawnFormationPreview` draw them in the editor. The **spawn effect configuration** (`CSpawnEffectConfiguration`, 15 members: `sfxc_fSpawnSourceStopChance`, `sfxc_idSpawnSourcePrepareAnimation`, `sfxc_idSpawnSourceAnimation`, an effect, its stretch and power, a sound, `sfx_fSpawnDelay`, a spawn projectile and its parameters, `sfxc_idSpawneeAnimation`) names an **effect played at the spawn source**, a **projectile that carries the spawnee** or an animation; `SpawnSourceAndSpawnEffect` (profiled) errors "Spawner that uses spawn source doesn't have spawn effect configuration defined!" when a spawn source has none.

### 6. The multiplier at boot (read in part, medium to low)

`OnBoot` (read): when `en_bSetIntitialValues` is set (every spawner of the 58 and 59 layouts) the initial total is `max(en_ctTotal, 1)` and the initial group `max(en_ctGroup, 1)` and the flag is cleared; the spawner then applies the tweaks and the multiplier and enters `SetState(0)`. **`DoMultiplier`** chooses by the class of the **spawnee**: one of seven procedures (`DoPuppetMultiplier` for the legged and generic legged characters, `DoAircraftMultiplier`, `DoTurretMultiplier`, `DoRollerBallMultiplier`, `DoHoverCraftMultiplier`, `DoMultiSpawnerMultiplier`; items and props get none): each sets the **launch cone**, the **health** (`en_iHealth` from the puppet parameters' health), `en_bCanFly` (from the parameters' "flying" flag), the initial delay (a random fraction times a constant: spreads co-op spawns) and `en_bNoMulti` (the spawner is not multiplied: a puppet named "Worm" or "Spawner" in `en_strSpawningPuppetName`, a record flagged in the parameters, or a tweak). `GetMultiplier` takes the session's enemy multiplier from the game info entity (`sgp_iEnemyMultiplier` and per player, `players-session-classes.md`). Level-specific cases in the code: `1_4_ApproachingCity` (rolling balls get a wide cone; the hovercraft case), `7_7_Mental` (turrets) and the three arena levels (no multiplier). `PrintSpawnerMultiplierInfo` (3 kB) prints the result to the console when `sam_ddc` is set (the lines "-- Spawner Multiplied --", "Line:", "Rect:", "Pie:", "Markers:" describe the formation chosen). **The multiplier arithmetic itself is not established** (Open question 2).

### 7. Multi-spawners, spawn delayers and spawn markers

- **`CMultiSpawnerEntity`** (60 in 15 worlds; entity members `en_hre`, `en_henSpawner` → the spawner it owns: all 60 are `SSpawnerEntity`): a spawner whose **spawnee is a template picker**: its record `SMultiSpawnerProperties` (`ep_hspSpawner`: set in the 60 owned records, null in the 60 world records; `ep_hstpTemplateProvider`: a `CSpawnTemplateProvider` in all 120; `ep_bRandomizeTemplateOrder`: 1 in 114 of 120; `ep_fRndBase`: 28 distinct values) holds a **list of weighted spawn templates** (`CWeightedSpawnTemplate`: 3 members). `GetPropertiesToSpawn` (name) returns the template for the next spawnee (randomised or in order), `UpdateSpawnTemplates` (name) refreshes the spawner's copy, `GetSpawneeCountLimitType` reads the template (profiled: `CanBeKicked` of the chosen template decides the limit class), `BreakSpawnerLinks`/`BreakMultiSpawnerLinks`/`GetEffectiveEntity` serve the editor. `ContributeToGameTotalStats` (read) adds the multi-spawner's **template count** to the level's enemy total (the "kills" denominator), and `CSpawnerEntity::ContributeToGameTotalStatsAsDeathSpawner` warns "Multispawner must not be used as death spawner!". A multi-spawner **must not be a death spawner**.
- **`CSpawnDelayerEntity`** (binary only, run-time): created by `DoSpawnEffect` through `SpawnEntity(CSpawnDelayerProperties)`: members `en_fSpawnDelayTime`, `en_henSpawner`, `en_iSpawnee`, `en_qvSpawnLocation`, `en_vVelocity`; `OnCreate` schedules the event "SpawnIt" after the delay and `SpawnIt` tells the spawner to make spawnee number `en_iSpawnee` at the stored location and velocity (profiled). Properties members: `ep_fSpawnDelayTime`, `ep_henSpawner`, `ep_iSpawnee`, `ep_vVelocity`.
- **`CSpawnMarkerEntity`** (177 in 16 worlds; members `.1`, `.2` two editor renderables; no record members): a **plain marker** entity with a placement (`SetAbsPlacement`, `GetAbsPlacement`, `GetBoundingBox`); spawners with a `CMarkersFormation` or `SMarkersLauncher` take its placement as a spawn point (name; low: the link from the formation to the markers is by handle in the formation, not read).

## Invariants

1. 3,783 of 3,783 spawners have `.120` an entity with a world record, `.21` a record that is not in the world list, and an owned `SSpawnerProperties`; each `SSpawnerProperties` is referenced exactly once (script and `wld-entities.md`).

4. The 60 multi-spawners each own exactly one spawner; the spawner's `ESpawneeCountLimitType` is 2, 3 or 4 for every spawner and 4 for every item and prop (script cross-tabulation).
