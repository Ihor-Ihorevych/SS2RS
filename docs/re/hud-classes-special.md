## Summary

The 18 elements that are **not** simple are the ones that draw something with its own layout: the **weapon selection** (a vertical list, a horizontal list and a radial menu), the **Netricsa** panel and its icon, the **scanner**, the **damage** indicator, four **screen effects and single messages** (freezing, auto-aim target, saving, object action), six **message queues** (conversation, item picked, enemy purged, extra life, frags counter, time remaining) and the empty **background template**. Together with the two elements of `players-session-classes.md` (player death, voting) they are the 20 direct children of `CHudElement`. They share the 11 virtual slots; each overrides `PrepareRenderingParameters` and `Render`, a few add `Initialize`, `Reset`, `OnHudCommand` and their own methods. **83 named methods** are exported by these 18 classes (the page lists every one).

## Layout / rules

### 1. Weapon selection (3 elements)

The three share two arrays of `WeaponIndex` (two rows, one value per slot; -1 = empty) and the rule that the **first row wins**: a slot shows the first weapon if the player has it, else the second; if only the second row's weapon is owned it moves up to the first row (`PrepareWeaponsArrays`, **R**). The default slot table is in `hud-classes.md` test vector 5. The row arrays are 10 entries long.

## Invariants

1. The 18 classes have the parent `CHudElement` (14 by registration, 4 by virtual table: `CAutoAimHudElement`, `CFreezingHudElement`, `CObjectActionHudElement`, `CSavingHudElement`), and each has 11 or more slots; the weapon, Netricsa and queue elements keep slots 0 to 10 in place and add after 10.
2. A slot command number outside 1 to `GetCountOfWeaponSlots` is ignored by the lists; outside 11 to 18 by the menu; outside 19 to 23 by Netricsa.
3. A weapon slot change never selects a weapon the player does not own (both rows are tested before the change request).
4. A message queue element draws at most one text at a time.

## Test vectors

1. **Slot command.** With a player who owns the Colt and the zap gun and not the double shotgun: command 2 selects the zap gun (slot 2, first row); command 3 selects the auto shotgun only if owned, else does nothing; command 10 is ignored when `ggp_cwuComboWeaponsUsing` is 2 (nine slots).
2. **Weapon menu.** Command 13 (`HC_WEAPON_DOWN`) = direction 2: the inner ring's entry for that direction if owned and not selected, else the outer one.
3. **Frags counter.** `OnFragsCountChanged(3)` in a deathmatch shows the text `3 frags left`; 6 shows nothing; 0 shows nothing.
4. **Player death.** Seven messages added within 3 s: the first is dropped when the seventh arrives (`pdhep_ctMaxDeaths` = 6).
