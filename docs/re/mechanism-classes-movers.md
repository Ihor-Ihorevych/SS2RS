## Summary

The page covers **19 exported classes** of the moving and colliding level parts: `CDoorEntity`/`CDoorProperties`, `CAnimatedMoverEntity`/`Properties`/`EnvelopeProvider`, `CPlatformEntity`/`Properties`, `CPortalEntity`/`Properties`, `CBouncerEntity`/`Properties`, `CBarrierFieldEntity`/`Properties`, `CGenericMotorEntity`/`Properties`, `CExternalJointEntity`/`Properties` (the joint the data calls `CTestJointEntity`) and `CDiamondHandEntity`/`Properties`. In the **59 worlds** they are **1,553 placed entities** (barrier fields 825, animated movers 277, doors 176, test joints 93, bouncers 71, portals 54, generic motors 43, diamond hands 14) in addition to the 25,383 static models of `wld-geometry.md`. **`CPlatformEntity` is in no file** (the class exists in the binary only; its members are in section 4; Open question 1).

What a reader needs to know first:

1. **Every placed mechanism is a pair** (entity in the object list, property record in the extra section) and the two carry the same design numbers. Of the 1,553 entities **every one has a record** of the matching class (script, section 3), and the record's values equal the entity's (the entity holds the run-time copy).
2. **A mover is a model plus a kinematic body that is driven by an animation of the model.** Doors and animated movers play animations of their model or of an animation configuration (profiled: the door and mover functions call the engine's play-animation and animation-length functions and no path function). The data stores **identifiers of animations** (numbers), not names.
3. **`SBaseEntity.1` is the chapter link** of every `S`-layer entity: a handle to a `CChapterInfoEntity` (`mechanism-classes.md` section 2). A door, mover, portal, bouncer or barrier field that carries one is tied to a chapter. Counts per class below.
4. **Net behaviour** (`replication.md` 3.4, confirmed here by a second reading of the exported `GetNetBehavior` functions): doors, animated movers, barrier fields, bouncers, portals, cameras and the diamond hand are `NEB_STATIC` (1); the **platform, the generic motor and the external joint are `NEB_DYNAMIC` (2)**. Only the host decides; the clients receive the replicated members listed per class.
5. **Machine-generated state** (`en_*Net*`, handles of renderables, bodies and sound sources) is stored in the worlds too, with its start values: do not interpret it (the handles are object references of the file, not entity ids).

**Role.** A trigger volume that **throws whatever touches it**: a launch pad (`ep_fBounceSpeed` 30 to 110 m/s in the data) or a deflector, with an optional **air control** period after the launch (the touched puppet may steer in the air).

**Members of `CBouncerProperties` (12, all named in the data) and the entity copies (19).**

| Member | Values in the 71 records | Meaning |
|---|---|---|

| `ep_fNormalComponentMultiplier`, `ep_fParallelComponentMultiplier` | 0 in 70 (one 0.6) / 0 in 71 | how much of the incoming velocity is reflected along the bounce direction (negated) / kept across it (read: see the formula) |
| `ep_fMaxExitSpeed` | 200 in 46; 10 .. 100 | cap of the speed after the bounce |

The entity adds `en_hre`, `en_hre1`, `en_hre2` (editor renderables), `en_hhu` (the hull), `en_henLastBounced`, `en_tmLastBounce`, **`en_fIgnoreSameEntityPeriod` (0.1 s, all 71)**, `en_vBounceDir` (the direction from heading and pitch), `en_henParent`, `en_bActive` (1 in all 71).

**Rule (`OnHullTouched`, read, medium).** The same entity touching again within `en_fIgnoreSameEntityPeriod` is ignored. Otherwise the toucher's velocity *v* is split along the bounce direction *d* (unit length) into a **normal part** *vn* (along *d*) and a **tangential part** *vt* = *v* - *vn*, and the new velocity is **`vt * en_fParallelComponentMultiplier - vn * en_fNormalComponentMultiplier + en_fBounceSpeed * d`**; if its length exceeds `en_fMaxExitSpeed` it is scaled down to that length. The toucher's velocity is **set** to the result by a call that also passes the air control time, the air control factor, `en_fAirControlVelocityMultiplier` and a flag 1. (When the direction vector is shorter than a tiny constant the incoming velocity is replaced by zero before the formula, so the result is the bounce velocity alone.) All launch pads of the data have both multipliers 0 (70 of 71 and 71 of 71), so the incoming velocity is dropped and the exit velocity is exactly `en_fBounceSpeed` along *d* (high for the data, medium for the formula).

It can be **disabled and enabled** by script (`Disable`, `Enable`), and a spawner tweak can change a spawnee's barrier collision mode (`CSpawnerTweakBarrierCollision`).

**Members.** `SBarrierFieldProperties` (5): `ep_pmcModel` (a model configuration: **822 inline, 3 external `House_01_StairsBarrier.mdl`**), `ep_idParentPart` (0 except 2), `ep_vStretch`, **`ep_bIgnoredByNavigation`** (`.4`, 1 in 42 of 825: the field is not an obstacle for the path-finder: name from the binary, meaning from the name, low), `ep_bCustom` (1 in 14). `SBarrierFieldEntity` (8; **1 replicated**: `en_bNetEnabled`): `en_hmrModel` (editor model renderable), `en_hre`, `en_hhuHull` (the model hull: set in 825), `en_penParent` (null 667; static model 110; animated mover 48), `en_idParentPart`, **`en_bEnabled`** (1 in all 825), `en_bNetEnabled#` (1), `en_bCustom` (1 in 14). ### 8. Generic motors and external joints: `CGenericMotorEntity`, `CExternalJointEntity` (data `CTestJointEntity`)

**Generic motor** (43 in 6 worlds; properties 5 members): a **physics motor between two bodies** that a script drives. Members of the properties: `.1` source entity (a prop in 42 of 43), `.3` its part (0 in 39), `.2` destination entity (null in 42), `.4` its part, `.5` the joint template (`CGenericMotorJointTemplate`, 43). The entity (11) adds the joint (`CGenericMotorJoint`), a joint renderable, two placements and the **current command values** (`CGenericMotorJointValues`: 4 values per axis kind). **Methods (34):** the 20 script commands that set one value of the motor (the call to the joint's setter and a wake-up of the bodies, `ActivateBodies`: profiled; the meanings come from the names): `SetPositionX/Y/Z/H/P` (target position or angle), `SetNoDesiredPosX/Y/Z/H/P` (stop holding a target), `SetVelocityX/Y/Z`, `SetAngVelocityH/P`, `SetForceX/Y/Z`, `SetTorqueH/P` and the query `macGetLinearVelocity` (relative linear velocity of the two bodies); plus `LinkTargets` (read: resolves the two parts of the source and destination entity and initialises the joint between them; with only one entity given it joins it to the world), `CopyFromTemplate`, `CopyToTemplate`, `OnCreate`, `OnBoot`, `OnUpdate` (re-links when the targets change), `OnDelete`, `PreSendUpdate`, `PostReceiveUpdate` (a client sets the joint values it received), `CollectForSortedIteration`, `GetUpdatePriority` (by the distance to the closest player), `GetRelevantDistance`, `GetNetBehavior` (**`NEB_DYNAMIC`**). The five axes are the three positions and the heading and pitch angles; **no roll axis** exists (no `SetPositionB` / `SetTorqueB`).

**External joint** (data `CTestJointEntity`: 93 in 16 worlds; `CTestJointProperties` 7 members): a **passive joint between two bodies** that can break. Properties (`ep_hepSrc`, `ep_idSrcPart`, `ep_hepDst`, `ep_idDstPart`, `ep_pjtJoint`, `ep_idParentPart`, `ep_fBreakForce`): source is a prop in 21, a static model in 6, null in 66; destination a prop in 56 (also a serious bomb item, a damage power-up, XL health and XL armor once each: such items can hang on a joint), null in 33; the joint template is a **ball joint in 69**, a fixed joint in 16, a hinge in 1, none in 7 (the entity: 76 ball, 16 fixed, 1 hinge); **`ep_fBreakForce` is -1 (unbreakable) in 76**, 5,000 in 8, 50 in 4, 10 in 1 .. 1e7 in 2. **Methods (17):** `Link` (store the two entities and parts and link), `LinkTargets` (3.2 kB: resolves the targets, sets the joint's placement and the limits `SetMaxLinVel`, `SetMaxAngVel`, `SetMaxForce`, `SetMaxTorque`), `Unlink`, `OnCreate` (builds the ball joint and sets the break force), **`OnJointOverload`** (read: when the engine reports that the joint is overloaded, the entity breaks the joint and raises the **macro event "Broken"**), `ExecuteDestruction` (name: the destruction path of `destruction.md` calls it), `CopyFromTemplate`, `CopyToTemplate`, `OnBoot`, `OnUpdate`, `OnDelete`, `PreSendUpdate`, `PostReceiveUpdate`, `CollectForSortedIteration`, `GetUpdatePriority`, `GetRelevantDistance`, `GetNetBehavior` (**`NEB_DYNAMIC`**, 10 of 13 members replicated: `replication.md`).

### 9. The diamond hand: `CDiamondHandEntity`, `CDiamondHandProperties`

**Methods (24).** `OnCreate` (loads the three models), `OnUpdate` (rebuilds the aspects when the record changes), `SetupAspects` (4.2 kB: creates the hand, diamond and beam renderables, the "solid" mechanisms with the animation "Opening", the flare), `DeleteAspects`, `OnStep` (profiled: opens or closes the hands by the **ratio of health** and the oscillator, evaluates the "Opening" animation, fires the **macro events "Activated", "Deactivated" and "CircleClosed"**; `OpenedHandOscilator` fires "HalfOpened"), `SetupOpenedAnimationRatio`, `OnReceiveDamage`, `GetHealthRatio`/`SetHealthRatio`, `Disable`, `ConnectBeamWithEntity` (places and stretches a beam model from a hand to the next), `GetRandomBeamPassingDiamond` (only valid on the **first diamond of a chain**: otherwise the warning "Function GetRandomBeamPassingDiamond() can be called only for first diamond in chain!"), `HideBeams`/`UnhideBeams`, `OnBoot`, `OnShutDown`, `OnDelete`, `GetAbsPlacement`, `GetNetBehavior` (`NEB_STATIC`), `GetRelevantDistance`, `GetUpdatePriority`, `PreSendUpdate`, `PostReceiveUpdate`.

## Invariants

1. Every placed door, animated mover, barrier field, bouncer, portal, generic motor, external joint and diamond hand has exactly one property record of the matching class; the 1,553 entities and 1,553 records agree (script section 3: 176, 277, 825, 71, 54, 43, 93, 14).
2. All 176 doors are in `EDoorState` 1 and unlocked at rest in the file; all 277 animated movers are in state 0; all 825 barrier fields are enabled; all 71 bouncers are active (`en_bActive` 1).
3. All 54 portals carry a chapter link; no bouncer, barrier field, generic motor or joint does.
4. `ep_idCloseAnim` is 0 in every record (176): a door's closing is not driven by a stored close animation id.
5. Launch pads: both multipliers are 0 in 70 of 71 bouncers.
6. `CPlatformEntity` and `CPlatformProperties` are in no file: a reader must not look for them in a world.
7. The net members of the mechanisms (`en_*Net*`) are at their start values in every placed entity (0, or 1 for the enabled flags and the door state); unlike the mission objectives (`mechanism-classes-triggers.md`) no stray value occurs.
