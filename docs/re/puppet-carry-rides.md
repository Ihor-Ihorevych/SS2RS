## Summary

1. **Carrying** (the "use" key of the player, section 1): a puppet can hold **one physics entity** in front of it with a **manipulator joint**; the held object is pulled to a point **2 m in front of the eye along the look direction** (1 m when winding up a throw), the primary fire button **winds up** (press) and **throws** (release; **full charge throws by itself**), the throw is an **impulse** `mass × speed` with a speed from **1 to 30 m/s** (default limits) that grows with the hold time up to **0.55 s** and an impulse cap of **mass limit × top speed** (300 for the defaults). Only the **player brain** starts a carry (`TakeObject` has no other caller in the DLL, **medium**).
2. **Seats** (section 2): the **eight seat flags** are all read somewhere; section 2.1 lists the reader of each. The player's seat search (`FindBestSeat`) picks the free seat with the best **distance-and-aim score** inside **8 m** (global limit) and a cone that narrows from 80 degrees to 20 degrees with distance.
3. **Rides** (section 3): every simulation step a puppet runs **`ProcessRideRiderRelations`**, which **registers it in its ride's rider list as soon as its ride link is set** (not when the climb ends: this corrects `brain-rides.md` section 2), removes dead or departed riders (`RiderLost`), raises **`AllRidersSittingInRide`** and the **turned on / off** transitions of the ride (the operator seat is occupied or not), and starts and stops the **riding state** notifications.
4. **Default riders** (section 4): a ride can carry **mounted riders** created at boot from the parameter file (`albinowithchick` with a `HellChick`) or from the placed instance; they sit at once, without walking.
5. **Auto-fix** (section 6): a tilted ride (pitch or bank beyond 80 degrees) can be **righted** by a balance joint for up to **10 s**; the state ends earlier when the **doubled** pitch and bank are within 80 degrees (that is, **within 40 degrees**), which differs from the 80-degree test of `IsRideFixed`.

## Layout / rules

### 1. Carrying objects

**1.1 Members and parameters (**high** for names, **medium** for use).** Puppet members: `en_henCarrying` (the carried entity), `en_hboCarrying` (its body), `en_iCarriedPartIndex` (which mechanism part), `en_hjoCarrying` (the manipulator joint), `en_hmechCarried` (its mechanism, for the ignore list), `en_qvGrabRel` (the grab placement in the body's frame: a quaternion and a position), `en_tmFire0Pressed` (time the primary button went down), `en_ubLastSeenFireButtonStates` (the four button bits of the previous step). Parameters: `pp_omObjectManipulation` = two records of **`CObjectManipulationParams`** (= the data's `CThrowingParams`, `helper-classes-params.md` 6): `om_ompNormal` and `om_ompOnProteins`, each, in order: **max carrying mass** (default 100), **min grab velocity** (5), **max grab velocity** (20), **max throw velocity mass** (10), **min throw velocity** (1), **max throw velocity** (30). The data keeps the defaults in 245 to 252 of 252 instances; the others are 1,000, 10,000 and 201 (the two mass limits, large characters), 10 (min grab) and 50 (max throw). The proteins set is chosen when `IsOnProteins` is true (a puppet-class stub answering **no**; the player puppet answers "power-up 0 is active", `players-session-classes.md`).

Global parameters: `ggp_fMinObjectShootoutTime` **0.05 s** and `ggp_fMaxObjectShootoutTime` **0.5 s** (data: one value each, `rsc-ctl-schema.md`).

**1.2 The use key (`CPlayerBrainEntity::OnUse`, host; **medium**).** On a client the call is forwarded to the host (a remote call). On the host, with the player's puppet `P` and the entity `E` the player is aiming at (`use target`, a member of the brain):
1. `P` is **riding**: the seat data of the ride is looked up; if its flags contain **`SF_PLAYER_CANT_EXIT` (128)** nothing happens, else the brain's `ExitFromRide` runs (`brain-rides.md` 5);
2. else `P` is **carrying** an entity: `DropObject`;
3. else if there is no use target: nothing;
4. else with the target's `GetObjectManipulationFlags`: **bit 1 (use)**: the target's "use" virtual is called, the world info's "last used by" member is set to `P`, and a script event is sent to the target (event identifier not recovered); **bit 2 (take)**: `TakeObject` with the **hull the aim ray hit** and the hit placement, **unless** the target is a **legged puppet** in the pose **`PP_STAND` (1) or `PP_FALL` (6)** whose `en_bLastTouchedFloor` is **0** (then nothing is taken and the code goes on to the ride branch; **medium-low**: the member identities of the test were matched by position); otherwise, if the target is a **ridable puppet** (`IsRidable`), the target's **`FindBestSeat`** (section 2.2) is asked with the player's view origin and, on success, the brain's **`PossesRide(ride, seat, way 0, 1, 1)`** is called (`brain-rides.md` 2: the last flag 1 means a tilted ride is **auto-fixed**; the teleport decision is the player brain's own).

**`GetObjectManipulationFlags` of a puppet** is **9 (use + show message) when `PNF_CAN_BE_USED` is set** (bit 4 of `en_pnfNetFlags`; `EnableUsage` sets it, `DisableUsage` clears it) and **0** otherwise, so only a puppet that was made usable shows the use prompt; the prompt text of a ridable puppet is `Press {plcmdUse} to enter` when the ride is fixed (`IsRideFixed`) and `Press {plcmdUse} to fix` when it is not (a puppet without seats uses the base class text). The weapon-select bar and the player's "can be used for firing" are other pages.

**1.3 `TakeObject(hull, placement)` (**medium**).** Does nothing unless the puppet has its parameters, is **not already carrying** (neither body nor entity), and the hull has a **body whose owner entity exists and is not the entity the puppet is standing on** (`en_hboFloor`). Then: `DeleteCarryingJoint` (clears stale state), **`en_qvGrabRel` := the given grab placement converted into the body's frame** (the inverse of the body's placement applied to it; the rotation normalized, **identity when its squared length is 0 or less**), `en_henCarrying` := the owner entity, `en_iCarriedPartIndex` := the hull's part index, then the virtual **`OnObjectTaken`** hook (empty in the base class). The **joint is not created here**: the next step's `HandleObjectCarrying` does it. The sound and animation of the grab (`OnObjectGrab`: scheme sound **`ObjectGrab`** on the mouth sound source, scheme animation `ObjectGrab` played with a blend of `en_fAnimLerpTime` if the model has it) are played by `ConnectToCarriedObject`.

**1.4 `ConnectToCarriedObject` (called by the step when `en_hjoCarrying` is empty and `en_henCarrying` is set; **medium**).** Takes the carried entity's mechanism and the part `en_iCarriedPartIndex`; the part's body must exist, it becomes `en_hboCarrying`. If the virtual hook "ignore carried" (a base stub answering **0**) answers 0 **and** the puppet has a mechanism, the puppet's mechanism **ignores collisions with the carried mechanism** (ignore list; `en_hmechCarried` remembers it). With `P` the normal or proteins parameter set:
- `r = clamp(carried mass / P.max carrying mass, 0, 1)` (the mass is the entity's `GetMass`);
- max **linear velocity** of the joint = **`P.max grab velocity + (P.min grab velocity − P.max grab velocity) × r`** (20 at mass 0, 5 at the mass limit with the defaults); max **angular velocity** = **4π (12.566371) rad/s**; max **force** = **`P.max carrying mass × 60`**; max **torque** = **force × 0.02**;
- the joint is initialised between the puppet's body and the carried body, with the desired placement = the grab placement `en_qvGrabRel` (translated by the body's rotation);
- then the carried entity is told it was taken (virtual `OnBeenTaken`), `OnObjectGrab` plays, and the script event **`ObjectTaken`** is sent to the puppet.

**1.5 `HandleObjectCarrying` (every step, after the operator input and before the damage reset; **medium**).**
1. No joint but a carried entity: `ConnectToCarriedObject`.
2. A carried body exists: it is told (virtual) to stay awake/active. If the body or the entity is **missing** while a joint exists: `DeleteCarryingJoint`.
3. **If the carried entity is the thing the puppet stands on** (the owner of `en_hboFloor`): `ThrowObject(0)` (it is dropped).
4. With joint and entity: the **look direction** (heading `H`, pitch `p`) is read; **`p` is capped at 75 degrees (1.3089969 rad)**; `d = 2.0` m; **if the primary fire button bit is held** (`en_ubLastSeenFireButtonStates` bit 0) the winding ratio `w = CalculateObjectThrowingRatio()` is computed and `d = 2.0 − w` (**2 m down to 1 m**), and when `w` is **exactly 1.0** the **auto-throw** is requested (point 6). The desired point = the view origin (`GetViewOrigin`, the eye) + `d` × forward, with forward `(−sin H cos p, sin p, −cos H cos p)`; the desired orientation is the look orientation; both are expressed **relative to the puppet's body** and given to the joint (`SetDesiredPlacements`).
5. Joint limits are refreshed each step: max force = `P.max carrying mass × 60`; **max torque = the same value, but 0 when the carried mass is at least the mass limit**; when the object is **not** heavy and its manipulation flags have **bit 4 (disable centering)** clear the desired orientation offset is zeroed (so the object is "centered" in front); a heavy object is not rotated and the flag-4 object keeps its own orientation. (**medium-low**: the torque and offset rules of the last two clauses come from the order of the calls; the joint physics is not read.)
6. **Auto-throw at full charge:** if an operator (a player brain) exists, the primary button is **released** (`OnFireButtonReleassed(0)`) and `ThrowObject(1.0)` runs.

**1.6 The buttons (`ExecuteOperatorFiring`, once per step when an operator exists and the puppet is not riding or `CanFireFromOwnWeapons`; **medium**).** For each of the **four** fire buttons `i` (0 primary, 1 secondary, 2, 3) it compares the operator's current button bits with the previous step's (`en_ubLastSeenFireButtonStates`; the stored value is then replaced by the operator's). While the operator has marked a button as **consumed**, nothing happens for that button; the mark is cleared on the button's release edge. `i` is mapped: for `i` 0 and 1 and when a class stub says so (answers **no** in the base class), the two are **swapped**. Then:
- **held**: `OnFireButtonDown(i)`;
- **pressed edge**: for `i = 0` `en_tmFire0Pressed` := now; with **no carried entity or `i ≠ 0`**: `OnFireButtonPressed(i)` (a weapon's fire key); **else (carrying, primary)**: **`OnObjectThrowPrepare`** (scheme sound `ObjectThrowPrepare`, scheme animation of that name, blend `en_fAnimLerpTime`);
- **released edge**: with no carried entity or `i ≠ 0`: `OnFireButtonReleassed(i)`; **else**: **`ThrowObject(CalculateObjectThrowingRatio())`**.
(`OnFireButtonDown` in puppet state 6, consecutive shooting, instead records `en_tmAttackDesired`; `OnFireButtonPressed` calls the puppet's `DoAttack(button, 0)` and `Released` its `StopAttack`: `puppet-attacks.md`.)

**1.7 `CalculateObjectThrowingRatio` and `ThrowObject(ratio)` (**medium**).**
- **Ratio** `w = clamp((now − en_tmFire0Pressed − ggp_fMinObjectShootoutTime) / ggp_fMaxObjectShootoutTime, 0, 1)` = **0 up to 0.05 s of holding, 1 from 0.55 s** (the divisor is the maximum itself, not the difference). **Quirk (read):** when the puppet is **on proteins** the function first **copies the six members of the proteins record over the normal record** in the shared parameter object, so the normal values stay changed after the power-up ends and for every puppet that shares the file (**medium**: the copy was read once; whether the original ever restores them was not found).
- **`ThrowObject(w)`:** needs the parameters, a carried entity and a carried body. Let `m` = the entity's mass, `P` the normal or proteins set. **Application point:** if `P.max carrying mass ≤ m` **or** the entity's manipulation flags have **bit 4**, the point is the **grab point** (the body's position plus the body's rotation applied to the stored grab position: the impulse **spins** the object); otherwise the **body's position** (centre). Then `DeleteCarryingJoint` (the script event **`ObjectThrown`** is sent here, **before** the impulse). If the body still exists: forward `f = (−sin H cos p, sin p, −cos H cos p)` from the look direction (**no 75-degree cap here**), the magnitude is **`min((P.min throw velocity + (P.max throw velocity − P.min throw velocity) × w) × m, P.max throw velocity mass × P.max throw velocity)`**, the body gets **`AddImpulse(point, f × magnitude)`** and `OnObjectThrow` runs (scheme sound **`ObjectThrow`**, animation `ObjectThrow` with **no blend**).
  With the defaults (1, 30, mass limit 10): `w = 0` throws at 1 m/s, `w = 1` at 30 m/s, and an object heavier than 10 is capped at an impulse of **300**, i.e. `300 / m` m/s.

**1.8 `DeleteCarryingJoint` / `DropObject` (**medium**).** `DropObject` is the same function as `DeleteCarryingJoint` for the puppet: it clears `en_hboCarrying` and `en_henCarrying`; **if a joint existed**: the ignore-list entry is removed (when the same stub answers 0), the joint is deleted, the script event **`ObjectThrown`** is sent, the carried entity is told it was thrown (virtual `OnBeenThrown`) and the hook **`OnObjectDropped`** runs (the **player's** version selects the right-hand weapon again and, when a left-hand weapon is remembered, that too: `puppet-tools.md` 7). `OnShutDown` and `DoDying` both call it, so a puppet that dies or is removed **drops** what it carries. `macThrowObject(ratio)` is `ThrowObject` for scripts, `macGetThrownObject` returns `en_henCarrying`. One more call of the `ThrowObject` slot is made inside `OnReceiveDamage` on an entity whose identity was not resolved (open question 2).

### 2. Seats

**2.1 Seat flags and where each is read (**medium**, flags **high**).** `SeatFlags` of `psd_sfSeatFlags` (`puppet-vehicle-classes.md`); value, name, **reader**:

| Value | Flag | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `SF_RIDER_CAN_SHOOT_OWN_WEAPON` | `CanFireFromOwnWeapons` (3.4); the player's `IsAutoAimingSupported` (the seat without it asks the ride's parameters: a member of the ride's parameters decides) |
| 2 | `SF_WAY_POINT_SEAT` | `FindBestSeat` (skipped); the AI's `GetAvailableSeat` (`brain-rides.md` 1) |
| 4 | `SF_PUPPETS_CANT_SEAT` | **only** the AI's `GetAvailableSeat`; **not** `FindBestSeat` |
| 8 | `SF_RIDER_DIES_WITH_RIDE` | `DoDying` of the ride: a rider on a seat with it dies (`ApplyLethalDamage`) when the ride dies; without it the rider falls out (`DoFallOutFromRide`) (section 7) |
| 16 | `SF_PLAYER_CANT_SEAT` | `FindBestSeat` (skipped) |
| 32 | `SF_PLAYER_CAN_BE_DAMAGED` | the player puppet's `IsInvulnerableAsRider`: **invulnerable when the flag is clear** and the seat exists (a missing seat: not invulnerable) |
| 64 | `SF_NON_PLAYER_IS_INVOULNERABLE` | the base `IsInvulnerableAsRider`: **invulnerable when set** |
| 128 | `SF_PLAYER_CANT_EXIT` | the player brain's `OnUse` (1.2) |

Seat data layout read by position (**medium**): name, attachment, **"operated from" seat** (`psd_idOperatedFromSeat`), flags, rider mechanism, sitting animation, moving animation, **approach-from seat**, the ride part to attach to, "rider can turn head"; ways to the seat follow. `GetSittingMechanism(seat)` is the seat's rider mechanism name, or, when that name is empty, the puppet's default sitting mechanism.

**2.2 `FindBestSeat(viewPlacement, out ride-and-seat, in/out score)` (**medium**; the player's seat choice).** Returns 0 at once unless **`ggp_fAllowedPlayerDistanceFromSeat` (8 m) is above 0**. `viewPlacement` is a quaternion and a position (the eye); `forward` = the quaternion rotating `(0, 0, −1)`. The out record's ride is set to this puppet (its **way defaults to 0**, its seat to invalid). For every seat `i` in table order:
1. skip it if its flags contain **2 or 16**;
2. skip it if **`GetRiderOnSeat(seat)`** finds a puppet: any member of **`en_eaRiders` or `en_eaDefaultRiders`** whose `en_idSeat` equals the seat name (so a seat reserved by a default rider is taken even before that rider sits);
3. `d` = the seat's world position − the eye, `dist = |d|`; skip when **`dist > 8`**; `t = dist / 8`; `dir = d / dist` (zero vector when `dist = 0`); `dot = dir · forward`;
4. cone: **`c = cos(80° − t × 60°)`** (1.3962635 and 1.0471976 rad; the cone half-angle is 80° at the seat and 20° at 8 m); skip when **`dot ≤ c`**; angular score `a = (dot − c) / (1 − c)` for `dot < 1`, **1** for `dot ≥ 1`;
5. **score = `(1 − t) + a`** (0 to 2); if it is **greater than the score in `*score`** (the caller's running best), the seat name and the score are stored and the function will return 1.
It returns 1 if any seat was stored. **`TryFindBestRideAndSeat`** (player brain) runs it for **every alive puppet of the world list** with a fresh score of 0 each and keeps the best-scoring ride and seat; it returns 1 when that score is above 0. Its callers were not found (open question 3); `OnUse` asks only the aimed target.

### 3. Riders and rides

**3.1 `ProcessRideRiderRelations` (every step of every puppet, early in `OnStep`; **medium**).** `R` = this puppet's `en_henRide`, `PLF_*` = `en_plfLocalFlags` (`puppet-classes.md` 3.3).
1. **Riding notifications.** With `riding = (R exists and en_rsRidingState = 3)`: if `PLF_CURRENTLY_RIDING` (1) is clear and `riding`: **`OnStartRiding`** (the puppet's virtual: below) and the brain's `OnStartRiding`; if it is set and not `riding`: **`OnStopRiding`** (the puppet's: sends the script event **`StoppedRiding`**) and the brain's. The flag := `riding`.
2. **Link.** `R` missing: `en_rsRidingState` := **0**. Else this puppet is **added to `R`'s `en_eaRiders` if not a member** (whatever the riding state: `RegisterForRide` sets the link, the next step adds the rider).
3. **Lost riders.** For this puppet as a ride: every entry of `en_eaRiders`: a deleted entity sends the script event **`RiderLost`** (the entry is dropped by the final clean-up); a live puppet that is **alive and whose `en_henRide` is this puppet** stays; every other (dead, or riding something else) is **removed from the list and `RiderLost` is sent** for it. Then null entries are cleared.
4. **Turned on/off.** `operator seat` = the ride's parameter `op_idOperatorSeat` (`SOperatingParams`, member `pp_opOperatingParams`; data: `empty` or `Seat`). `on` = **true** when the parameters are missing or the operator seat is the invalid identifier, else true iff **a rider whose `en_idSeat` is that seat has `en_rsRidingState` = 3**. `PLF_ON` (8) clear and `on`: virtual **`OnTurnedOn`** (calls the brain's `OnTurnedOn`, the moment `TurnedOn`); set and not `on`: **`OnTurnedOff`** (brain's `OnTurnedOff`, then **`StopMoving`**); the flag := `on`.
5. **All riding.** If the rider list is **not empty**: `all` = every rider exists and has state 3. `PLF_ALL_RIDERS_RIDING` (2) clear and `all`: the script event **`AllRidersSittingInRide`** is sent to the ride and its brain's **`OnAllRidersClimbed`** runs (`brain-rides.md` 4); the flag := `all`. **With an empty rider list the flag is left as it was** (so a ride that lost its last rider keeps the flag, and the next rider that boards does not raise the event again if the flag was still set: **medium**, from the shape of the function).

**3.2 `RegisterForRide(ride, seat, way)` / `UnregisterFromRide`** are in `brain-rides.md` 2 and 4; added here: `RegisterForRide` also calls the virtual **`StoreToolsBeforeRide`** when `CanFireFromOwnWeapons` is false (`puppet-tools.md` 7); `UnregisterFromRide` clears `en_henRide`, `en_idSeat` (invalid), `en_iWayToSeat` (-1), `en_rsRidingState` (0), runs `ProcessRideRiderRelations` for the **old ride** and for itself, then **`RestoreToolsAfterRide`**.

**3.3 Helpers (**medium**).** `IsConnectedToRide` = riding state **2, 3 or 4**; `GetCountOfRidersInRide` counts the members of `en_eaRiders` with state **3** (default riders included only when they are in that list: they are); `IsMyRider(entity)` = membership in `en_eaRiders`; `GetRide` = `en_henRide`; `DoStartRiding` sets state **3**; `DoStopRiding(ride := en_henRide)` stores it in `en_henCurrentClimbingRide` and calls `PlayExitingFromRideSequence(ride, ride, seat, 0)` (`brain-rides.md` 5). `OnStartRiding` (puppet): stops moving, plays the rider animation "not moving" with a blend, **copies the seat's absolute placement** to the puppet (`SetAbsPlacement`), attaches the rider's mechanism to the ride's mechanism at the seat's ride part (`CMechanism::Attach`), and sends two script events, **`StartedRiding`** (to the rider) and **`NewRider`** (to the ride; which of the two goes to which was matched by meaning, **medium-low**). `OnStopRiding` sends **`StoppedRiding`**.

**3.4 `CanFireFromOwnWeapons` (**medium**).** True unless the puppet rides, its seat data exists, the seat's **operated-from seat is the invalid identifier** and the seat has **not** `SF_RIDER_CAN_SHOOT_OWN_WEAPON`. (A rider on a seat that is operated *from another seat* may fire its own weapon; the driver seat may not unless flagged. In the data 6 seats have flag 1 (17 in five files, 81 in `albino`); `brain-rides.md` 8.) A puppet that cannot fire has its tools stored on boarding (3.2); the operator's fire buttons reach `ExecuteOperatorFiring` only when the puppet is not riding or this is true.

### 4. Default riders (`MountRiders`, `SetupDefaultRiders`, `MountRide`; **medium**, data **high**)

`SetupDefaultRiders` (virtual, called by `OnBoot`) does nothing for a puppet that has a **spawner** or is a multi-spawner template; otherwise it mounts the riders of the **parameter file** (`pp_hcmGenericMounts`, a handle to a `CCharacterMounts` list of `CCharacterMount`: character id, behavior id, seat id, `cm_pppPuppetParams`, `cm_pcbCharacterBehavior`) and then those of the **placed instance** (`en_hcpInstanceMounts`). **Data: one parameter file has any: `albinowithchick.ep` mounts one rider on `Seat` with the parameters `HellChick.ep`** (script section 6 of the tools script); 10 placed instances have mounts (`brain-rides.md` 8).

`MountRiders` per record: with the seat identifier **invalid** the ride's **first seat** is used (a ride with **no seats** prints `Riders can't be mounted onto ride (<name>) that has no seats` and stops); a named seat that does not exist prints `Can't find seat: "<seat>" to mount character "<character>" onto character "<ride>".` and skips the record. For a record with a **parameter pointer**: the **name of the parameter class has the tail `PuppetParams` removed and `CharacterProperties` appended** (a name without that tail does nothing, silently) and a **generic character** of that properties class is created at the seat's absolute placement with the mount's behavior (`samCreateGenericCharacter`; failure prints `Generic character "<name>" can't be created as mountee`). The new puppet gets **`en_bMountee` = 1** (`DeleteDefaultRiders`, at shutdown, deletes them), is appended to `en_eaDefaultRiders`, **registers** itself on the ride (the ride's virtual `RegisterForRide`), attaches its mechanism to the ride's, gets **riding state 3**, plays the not-moving rider animation **without a blend**, its brain's `OnStartRiding` runs and it is appended to the ride's `en_eaRiders`. Nothing walks and no animation sequence plays.

`MountRide(ride)` (script macro `macMountRide`, default seat the ride's first seat; `brain-rides.md` 2) does the same for an **existing** puppet: it needs both mechanisms and requires that the puppet's mechanism is **not already attached** to the ride's.

### 5. Rider animations (**medium**)

`PlayRiderAnim_RideNotMoving(immediately)`: only for a puppet with a ride, state 3 and a seat whose sitting animation (`psd_idRiderAnimRideNotMoving` / `psd_idSittingAnim`) is **not invalid**: a cloned animation state is started (blend **0** when `immediately`, else `en_fAnimLerpTime`), animation groups **1 and 2** are removed and the seat's animation is played in group 1 looping. `PlayRiderAnim_RideMoving`: the seat's **moving** animation (`psd_idRiderAnimRideMoving`) if valid: a cleared state with the lerp time, group 1 removed, the animation played in group 1. Each step the **ride's** `OnStep` compares `|en_vDesiredTempoAbs|` of this step with that of the previous step against **1e-5**: when it **rises above** 1e-5 every rider plays the **moving** animation, when it **falls to or below** it every rider plays the **not moving** one (blend `en_fAnimLerpTime`). `OnMyRideStartedMoving` and `OnMyRideStopped` do the same for one rider on demand. A client re-attaches a rider whose mechanism is not attached to the ride's in every `PostReceiveUpdate` (`AttachToRide`: the same attach as `OnStartRiding`, then the not-moving animation without blend).

### 6. Auto-fix of a tilted ride (**medium**; the pose maths **low**)

- **Trigger.** `AutoFixRide(fixer)` (called by `PossesRide` when the ride is not fixed and its last flag is set, `brain-rides.md` 2): stores the fixer in `en_henFixer` and sets the puppet state to **11 `PS_AUTO_FIXING_RIDE`**. `IsRideFixed`: the ride's absolute orientation as Euler angles; **fixed iff `|pitch| ≤ ggp_fWheeledVehicleFallOutPitch` and `|bank| ≤ ggp_fWheeledVehicleFallOutBanking`** (degrees, both 80; the numbers are converted to radians by π/180). The player's use prompt and `OnUse` use it (1.2).
- **`AutoFixingRideState`** (the state's think routine): creates once a **balance joint** (`en_hjoCorrection`), sets its limits to **150,000** and **`ggp_fWheeledVehicleCorrectionVelocity` (30 degrees/s, in radians)**, initialises it on the ride's body and gives it a desired upright orientation derived from the body's own orientation and, **when `en_henFixer` exists, the horizontal direction from the fixer to the ride** (without a fixer a default forward axis is used), then schedules **`FinishedRideFixing` after 10 s**. The rest of the pose maths (which axes, which sign) was **not** resolved (open question 2).
- **Ending.** `FinishedRideFixing` (after 10 s) deletes the joint and sets state **1 `PS_DEFAULT`**. **Earlier**, every step in state 11 the ride's `OnStep` tests **`|2 × pitch| ≤ 80°` and `|2 × bank| ≤ 80°`** (the two limits above, the angles **doubled** before the comparison, i.e. both within **40 degrees**); when true it deletes the joint and sets state 1. (**medium**: the doubling was checked in the instruction order of `OnStep`, and `IsRideFixed` has none, so the ride is "fixed" at 80 degrees but the fixing state only ends by itself at 40 degrees or after 10 s.)

### 7. Leaving, falling out, dying (**medium**)

`ExitAllRidersFromRide` → virtual `ExitAllRidersFromRideInternal`: for each rider **with a brain** the brain's `ExitFromRide` (`brain-rides.md` 5) is called. `FallOutAllRidersFromRide` → `FallOutAllRidersFromRideInternal`: **works on a copy of the rider list** and calls each live rider's **`DoFallOutFromRide`** = `UnregisterFromRide`, `SetDefaultMechanism`, and **state 1 (`PS_DEFAULT`) if the rider is alive**. `DoDying` (the ride): every rider in state 3 whose seat flags have **no bit 8** is told to `DoFallOutFromRide`; **with bit 8** it takes `ApplyLethalDamage` (arguments: an all-ones damage type and a zero vector), and then the ride drops what it carries. `OnShutDown`: `FallOutAllRidersFromRide`, the children deleted, the carried object dropped, `UnregisterFromRide` of the puppet itself, `en_eaRiders` cleared (a puppet that has a spawner or is a mountee is simply deleted).

### 8. Net behaviour (**medium**)

The replicated members of this page are `en_henRide`, `en_rsRidingState`, `en_idSeat`, `en_iWayToSeat` (`brain-rides.md` 7), the first two desired tools (`puppet-tools.md` 5) and `en_henToolEntityToPick`. The carry state (`en_henCarrying`, the joint) is **not** replicated: a client has no manipulator joint; the carried object moves through the host's physics and the normal entity updates. `OnUse` runs on the host (remote call); `ExecuteOperatorFiring`, and so `ThrowObject`, run where the operator's input is processed; the impulse is applied by whichever machine runs it (the physics is host-authoritative, `replication.md`).

### 9. Order inside `OnStep` of the parts on these pages (**medium**; refines `puppet-entity-classes.md` 5)

Observed order of the calls in one step: the floor-touch flag, **`ProcessRideRiderRelations`** (3.1), the idle and AI-enabled notifications, the operator's input and **`ExecuteOperatorFiring`** (1.6, only when not riding or `CanFireFromOwnWeapons`), the client position correction, healing and material damage, **`HandleObjectCarrying`** (1.5), the damage-accumulator reset (0.25 s after the last damage), the debug display, animation evaluation, the desired-velocity calculation, flocking, the feelers, the moving sounds, the altitude cap, the animation events, the movement lerp (`LerpToDesiredVelocityAndLook`), the **rider animation switch** (5), the **auto-fix test** (6), and **last** `HandleToolsActions` and `UpdateTools` (`puppet-tools.md` 3, 4), `CheckEngageFeelers` and the reset of the touch data. So a tool command given during step n is acted on by `UpdateTools` at the end of step n and by `HandleToolsActions` from step n+1.

## Invariants

1. A puppet carries **at most one** entity (`en_henCarrying`) and has at most one manipulator joint (`TakeObject` refuses while either is set).
2. The throw impulse is `min(v(w) × m, mass limit × top speed)` with `v` linear between the two throw velocities and `w` in [0, 1] (**medium**, 1 function).
3. A puppet is in the rider list of its ride from the step **after** `RegisterForRide`; it leaves it when it unregisters, dies, or its ride link differs (**medium**).
4. `FindBestSeat` never returns a seat that is a way-point seat, closed to the player, occupied (including default riders), farther than 8 m, or outside the cone; the returned way is 0 (**medium**).
5. Of the 22 rides in the data **no seat has flag 2 or 4** (`brain-rides.md` 8), so for them the seat flags that matter to the player are 1, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128: **6 seats can shoot** (flags 17 in 5 files and 81 in `albino`), **7 are closed to the player** (17 in 5, 16 and 81), **2 die with the ride and cannot be exited by the player** (136), **1 can damage the player** (32), **1 is non-player-invulnerable** (`albino`, 81).
