# Co-op lobby and session screens

`ss2_ui::lobby` (T10-018) holds the screens a player uses to **host or join a co-op game and wait for the others**: a start screen, the host's setup, three ways to join (the LAN list, a typed address, an invite code), the connecting screen, the lobby (players, characters, ready, start, leave, kick) and the error screens. It is a headless state machine like the menu: it takes `LobbyInput` and data updates, answers with a `LobbyCommand` for the app, and draws into a `DrawList` in canvas units (1920x1080) with the placeholder theme. **It knows nothing of the network and `ss2-ui` does not depend on `ss2-net`**: the app runs the scan, the session and the join, and tells the screens what happened with the plain types below. The text field is `data_wizard::TextField` (public there, reused as it is).

**Status: the screens work and are tested, but nothing feeds them from the real network layer yet** (see "Not covered").

## The flow

```text
           Back <-- Start --Host--> HostSetup --Start hosting--> Connecting --Connected--> Lobby --Leave--> Start
                       |                |  ^                       |  |  ^                   |
                      Join             Back                     Cancel  Failed            Failed (kicked, host left, link lost)
                       v                v                          |  v  |                   v
                      Join (LAN games | Direct address | Invite code) --Join--> Connecting       Error --Back--> where it came from
                                                                                                  `-- Try again --> Connecting
```

- **Start**: Host a game, Join a game, Back (`LobbyCommand::Back`, the app returns to its menu).
- **Host setup**: game name (a text field, 1 to 32 bytes after trimming; 32 characters at most in the field), players (left and right, or activate to cycle; between `LobbyConfig::min_players` and `max_players_limit`, default 2 to 16, starting at 4), a **password switch** and the password field (disabled, and skipped by the focus, while the switch is off; needed when it is on), the **level** chosen from the list given by `set_levels` (left, right or activate cycle through it), **Start hosting**. The start button is disabled, and a line under the form says why, while the name is blank or too long, there is no level, or the password is wanted and empty. It gives `LobbyCommand::Host(HostSettings { name, max_players, password, level })` and shows "Starting NAME".
- **Join**, three tabs (left and right switch the tab and move the focus with it; typing while a tab has the focus goes to that tab's field):
  - **LAN games**: the rows of `set_servers` (name, level, players n/m, ping, **Locked**, and "Full" or the reason it cannot be joined), sorted by ping (fastest first, no ping last), name or players with the **Sort by** button (`set_sort`), a **Refresh** button (`LobbyCommand::RefreshServers`; the app scans and calls `set_servers` and `set_scanning`). Six rows are drawn; **the list scrolls with the focus** and a scroll bar shows when there are more. A row that is full or incompatible is drawn dimmed and **skipped by the focus and by the pointer**. An empty list says "No games found on your network" and what to do instead (or "Searching the local network..." while `set_scanning(true)`). Activating a row joins it (`JoinTarget::Server(id)`).
  - **Direct address**: a text field; the text is read as you type (see below) and the line under it says what is wrong, or "OK: host, port"; **Connect** (and Enter in the field) is disabled until it reads.
  - **Invite code**: the same, for a code.
  - A **password** field is on all three (sent only when it is not empty), so a locked server from the list is joined by typing the password first.
- **Connecting**: the line "Connecting to ..." or "Starting ...", "Waiting for a reply" with moving dots, or, after `ConnectionState::Loading { what, percent }`, the step and a progress bar; **Cancel** (and Back) gives `LobbyCommand::Cancel` and returns to the form it came from. **`update(dt_ms)` gives up** after `LobbyConfig::connect_timeout_ms` (10 s) without an answer: it shows the "No answer" error and gives `Cancel` so that the app stops the attempt; `Loading` counts as an answer and restarts the clock.
- **Lobby**: **players** (a HOST tag, name, "(you)", character, Ready or Not ready, ping), **characters** to choose from (a character another player has is dimmed, says "Taken by NAME", and is skipped by the focus and the pointer; the own one says "Yours"), the **ready** button (`SetReady`; needs a character when there are characters to choose from), the **start** button (the host's; enabled only when every player, the host included, is ready) and **Leave**. Choosing a character gives `SelectCharacter(id)`, shows it at once, and **clears the local ready flag** (the session must clear it too: a new character is a new decision). The host can **remove a player**: the player rows take the focus only for the host (never the host's own row), a first press arms the kick ("Remove NAME from the game? Press again to confirm"), a second gives `Kick(id)`; moving the focus or Back cancels it (Back with a kick armed does not leave).
- **Error**: a title, what happened, what to do, **Try again** (only when sending the same request can help) and **Back** (to the form the request came from, or the start screen after a disconnect from the lobby). After a wrong password the Back lands on the password field.

Keys and pointer follow the menu: up and down move through the **usable** items and wrap; a pointer hover focuses a usable item; a press focuses it and the release activates only over the item it was pressed on; **pressing on a text field only focuses it**; a disabled item cannot be focused or pressed. Left and right: the cursor in a field, the value of the players and level rows, the tabs, from a player row to the first free character and back, and along the ready, start and leave buttons.

## What the app gives, and what it gets back

| Data in | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `set_servers(Vec<ServerRow>)` | `ServerRow { id, name, map, players, max_players, ping_ms, locked, status }` | the LAN list; `id` is an opaque key given back in `JoinTarget::Server`; `status` is `Open`, `Full` or `Incompatible(reason)` |
| `set_scanning(bool)`, `set_sort(ServerSort)` | | |
| `set_levels(Vec<LevelChoice>)` | `LevelChoice { id, name }` | the host's levels; the chosen one is kept when it is still in a new list |
| `set_characters(Vec<CharacterChoice>)` | `CharacterChoice { id: CharacterId(u32), name }` | |
| `set_players(Vec<PlayerRow>)` | `PlayerRow { id: PlayerId(u64), name, character, ready, ping_ms, host, local }` | the session's truth, replacing the list. A pending local character choice is shown until the list confirms it; if another player has it, the choice is dropped and a line says `<character> was taken by <player>: choose another`. **If the list has no host any more while the host was in the one before, the screens show the "host left" error.** An empty list is ignored for that. |
| `set_connection_state(ConnectionState)` | `Connecting`, `Loading { what, percent }`, `Connected`, `Failed(Failure)` | only the connecting screen and the lobby listen; an answer that arrives after the player cancelled is ignored |
| `update(dt_ms)` | | the dots, and the connect timeout |

`Failure` is `TimedOut`, `Unreachable`, `HostNotFound`, `InvalidInvite`, `BadPassword`, `ServerFull`, `Banned`, `NameTaken`, `InvalidName`, `Incompatible(text)`, `RetryLater { seconds }`, `Kicked(reason)`, `HostLeft`, `ConnectionLost`, `Other(text)`; each has a title, a message and a hint (`Failure::title`, `message`, `hint`) and `can_retry`.

| Command out | When |
|---|---|
| `Host(HostSettings)` | Start hosting; answer with `set_connection_state` (`Connected` once the host is in its own lobby) |
| `Join(JoinRequest { target, password })` | `target` is `Server(id)`, `Direct(DirectAddress { host: Ipv4 \| Ipv6 \| Name, port })` or `Invite(code)` |
| `RefreshServers`, `Cancel`, `SetReady(bool)`, `SelectCharacter(id)`, `Kick(id)`, `StartGame`, `Leave`, `Back` | |

### Mapping the `ss2-net` types (to be written in the app, T08-019's consumer)

- **`ServerRow`** from `discovery::ServerList::for_display()`'s `ServerEntry`: `id = entry.addr.to_string()`, `name`, `map`, `players`, `max_players`, `locked = info.password`, `ping_ms = Some((rtt_us / 1000) as u32)`, `status` = `Incompatible(compat.explanation())` when `compat` is not `Compat::Compatible`, else `Full` when `info.is_full()`, else `Open`.
- **`JoinTarget`** to a connection: `Server(id)` parses back to the `SocketAddr`; `Direct` is `DirectTarget::Addr` or, for `HostSpec::Name`, a name to resolve (an unresolved name is `Failure::HostNotFound`); `Invite(code)` goes to `discovery::decode_invite`, and `None` is `Failure::InvalidInvite` (the screens check the shape of a code, not its checksum). The password goes to `ClientConfig`.
- **`ConnectionState`** from `session::ClientState` and `join::JoinPhase`: `Connecting` is `Connecting`; `LoadingLevel`, `AwaitingBaseline`, `Transferring`, `CatchingUp` are `Loading` with a few words; `Connected` is `Connected`; `Refused(reason)` is `BadPassword`, `ServerFull`, `Banned`, `NameTaken`, `InvalidName`, `RetryLater { retry_after_ms.div_ceil(1000) }`, and `VersionMismatch`, `SchemaMismatch`, `ModMismatch` and `Incompatible(compat)` are `Incompatible(client.refusal_text())`; `TimedOut` is `TimedOut`; `Disconnected(Kicked(r))` is `Kicked(r)`, `Banned` is `Banned`, `ServerShutdown` is `HostLeft`; `reconnect::Stopped` is `ConnectionLost` or the refusal.
- **`LobbyCommand::Kick(PlayerId)`** to `SessionServer::kick(slot, reason)`; `Host` to a `ServerConfig` and an `Announcer` with the `ServerInfo` of the settings.
- **`PlayerRow`**: `ss2_net::session` knows peers, slots and names; **it has no ready flag or character**: those need a game-level message (not in the roadmap yet; follow-up T10-018b).

## Validation of what is typed

`parse_address` follows `ss2_net::discovery::parse_direct` (the rules of `docs/DISCOVERY.md`): `1.2.3.4`, `1.2.3.4:5`, `[::1]`, `[::1]:5`, a bare `::1`, `host`, `host:5`; a missing port is 27015; spaces around the text are trimmed, spaces inside are an error; the port is 1 to 65535 in digits; digits and dots that are not four numbers up to 255 are refused as an IPv4 address (not as a name); a name has labels of 1 to 63 letters, digits and dashes, not starting or ending with a dash, 253 bytes at most, and is lower-cased. Each refusal is an `AddressError` with a sentence. `normalize_invite` follows `decode_invite`'s reading (case, dashes and spaces do not matter; O, I, L read as 0, 1, 1), then requires 15 characters (IPv4) or 34 (IPv6) and the zero padding bits of the last character, and gives the code in its written form; 16 to 33 characters are "still on the way" and more than 34 is too long. The checksum is **not** checked here.

## Strings and allocation

Strings that vary (field texts, the sorted list, the player and server rows, the error sentences, the connecting lines) are built at run time: `items()`, `sorted_servers()`, `players()` and `draw_into` allocate short `String`s and `Vec`s each call. The screens are on for seconds and are not part of the frame budget (`docs/UI_BUDGET.md`); `draw_into` keeps the capacity of the list it is given.

## How it was tested

- **110 unit and property tests** in `crates/ss2-ui/src/lobby/tests.rs` (about 0.8 s), written together with the code and run as the code grew; seven are property tests (five on the text, 512 cases each: *any* text up to 400 characters, address-shaped and invite-shaped garbage, formatted IPv4 and IPv6 addresses reading back, and a normalised code reading back as itself, none may panic; two **random play** tests, 300 cases of up to 80 events each: keys, pointer events at random places, text, and every data update (servers, players, characters, levels, scanning, sort, connection states, time), with the invariants checked after **every** event: the focus is on a usable item that has a place on the screen, the scroll windows never pass the end of their lists, an armed kick implies the focus is on that player and the local player is the host, no two items overlap, every quad stays in the canvas, the error screen shows exactly when there is a failure, at most one quad has the focus colour, and the start button is usable exactly when `can_start()`). The rest cover every rule of the task: the three address forms and their refusals (IPv4, IPv6, host names, ports 0, 65535 and 65536, lengths 253 and 254, a 63 and a 64 character label), the invite code in any writing (case, dashes, spaces, O/I/L), each refusal reason and the padding bits, the focus skipping disabled rows (the password field, a full or incompatible server, a taken character, the start button, the connect button), the pointer rules, a list of 30 servers scrolled down and up, 15 players, 10 characters, a list that shrinks, a focused server that goes away or moves when the order changes, an empty list, a player who leaves while the focus is on them, a character taken by someone else while it is being chosen (and while it has the focus), the ready flag cleared by a new character, the start rule for a host and for a guest, the kick with its second press, the host leaving (by the list and by the state), kicks, time-outs and retries, and the drawing (every screen inside the canvas, text content, the blinking cursor, stars for passwords, the progress bar).
- **Mutation check** with `cargo mutants` on `lobby.rs` and `lobby/validate.rs` (386 mutants; `draw.rs` is covered by the goldens and was left out): **one third of them, 129 (shard 1 of 3), were run**, lib tests only, 90 s limit each, 29 minutes: **110 caught, 12 unviable (do not compile), 7 missed**. Of the seven, **four were killed by new tests** (the error screen's button placement, the number of player rows and of character rows that have a place at scroll 0, and a second host row in bad data being kickable), **one was an over-complicated line that I simplified** (`focus_on` and `move_focus` set a hint that `settle` sets again), and **two are equivalent** (`dir < 0` and `dir > 0` in `sideways`, where `dir` is only ever -1 or +1). The four fixed ones were re-run and are caught. **The other two thirds (257 mutants) were not run.**
- **Differential check against the network crate** (done once, with a temporary test that was **not committed**, because `ss2-ui` must not depend on `ss2-net`): `parse_address` and `ss2_net::discovery::parse_direct` agree (both read it, to the same address and port, or both refuse it) on 28 fixed texts, on 20,000 random address-shaped strings and on 20,000 random strings of any characters; every code `encode_invite` makes for random IPv4 and IPv6 addresses passes `normalize_invite` unchanged, and **every text `decode_invite` accepts passes `normalize_invite`** (20,000 cases): the shape check is never stricter than the real decoder. The two fixture codes in the tests are what `encode_invite` makes for `192.168.1.5:27015` and `[2001:db8::1]:1`.
- **Golden images**: seven screens at the five sizes (35 goldens, 18 to 172 KB each) in `crates/ss2-render/tests/golden_screens.rs`: start, host setup (password on, level picked), the LAN browser (seven servers: open, locked, full, incompatible, no ping, a cut name, a 1450 ms ping, a scroll bar), a direct address that does not read, connecting with a loading step, the lobby with four players in mixed states (one is the host, two ready, one with no character; two characters taken), and the timeout error. The first renders showed the ping colliding with the lock label, grey text unreadable on the orange focus row, and a ready button that said "Not ready" for the action of becoming not ready; all three are fixed.

## Not covered (follow-up T10-018b)

- **Nothing feeds the screens from the real network layer**, and nothing opens them: the app does not show the lobby, run the discovery scan, host a session or join one. The mapping above is untested prose; no code calls `ss2_net` from `ss2-ui` or from the app for this yet.
- **No ready flag or character in the session layer**: the lobby shows and sends them, but the protocol has nowhere to carry them.
- **No chat** (out of scope), **no real server browser transport** (no master server or invite relay exists; the list is the LAN scan only), **no player name editing** (the app gives the name to the session), **no host migration** (the host leaving ends the lobby), **no ban**, and **no ready check or countdown** before the start.
- **Placeholder look**: the menu's colours, no icons, no animation beyond the dots, no ping bars, no sound, **English strings only** (until T10-011), **no portrait layout** (T10-017b), no text measurement (names are cut by character count, so very wide letters can overflow a column), no gamepad on-screen keyboard for the text fields, no mouse wheel (the list scrolls with the focus) and no page up and down.
- **Passwords** are typed as `*` but held and handed over as plain strings; the app is expected to hand them straight to the session and not keep them.
- **Only the default 1920x1080 canvas was reviewed by eye**; the other four sizes are the same layout scaled (the goldens pass at all five).
